# Lucky Stone Website Design System

## Brand idea
**Good stays have a way of finding you.** Lucky Stone is a family-built collection of character-filled Southern homes. It should feel warm, colorful, capable, and personal—not like a hotel chain or a generic beige vacation-rental template.

## Visual language
- **Lucky pebbles:** asymmetrical rounded shapes, circular stamps, and curved cards reference smooth stones without becoming literal or rustic.
- **City-to-coast color story:** deep river navy anchors the brand; Gulf teal, porch-door yellow, coral sunset, and warm shell white provide energy.
- **Editorial photography:** full-bleed property photos, overlapping compositions, candid host imagery, and human-scale captions.
- **Friendly precision:** editorial Fraunces headlines paired with DM Sans interface type.

## Core colors
- River ink: `#0B2B40`
- Gulf teal: `#187F79`
- Front-door yellow: `#F3C84B`
- Sunset coral: `#F26B55`
- Shell: `#F7F1E7`
- Paper white: `#FFFDF8`
- Bayou green: `#1E5B4A`

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces 500–700, optical sizing on.
- UI/body: DM Sans 400–700.
- Large headlines use responsive `clamp()` sizing and tight tracking.

## Conversion structure
1. Emotional, distinct hero with all three locations represented.
2. Always-visible direct-booking CTA.
3. Search/date handoff to Hostaway.
4. Mood-based property discovery that scales to 25 homes.
5. Clear property facts and signature extras.
6. Direct-booking trust and secure-system explanation.
7. Verified guest social proof.
8. Family-host story and local authority.
9. Strong close with secure Hostaway booking.

## Scaling to 25 properties
Property cards support location and trip-style tags. In Squarespace, these can map to Summary Blocks or a collection with categories such as city, coast, game day, family, pet friendly, and large group. The mood buttons become collection filters rather than requiring a page redesign.

## Accessibility & performance
- Minimum 4.5:1 body-text contrast.
- Visible keyboard focus and skip link.
- Semantic headings, labels, buttons, and dialogs.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Native lazy loading for below-fold images during Squarespace implementation.
- Downloaded source photos should be resized to 1600–2200px WebP/AVIF before production upload.
